How to Convert CGPA to Percentage Accurately

Converting Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) into percentage is a common requirement for higher education admissions, government exam applications (like UPSC, GATE, SSC, CAT, SBI PO), job placements, and international transcript evaluation. Because different universities follow distinct grading standards (10-point, 9-point, 7-point, or 4-point scales), it is vital to apply the precise conversion multiplier sanctioned by your institution.

1. Standard 10-Point Scale Formula (× 10)

Most autonomous universities in India—including RTU Kota, SKIT Jaipur, MBM University Jodhpur, AKTU, Anna University, and SRM—adopt a direct linear conversion formula under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS):

Percentage (%) = CGPA × 10

Example: If a student achieves a CGPA of 8.45, the equivalent aggregate percentage is 8.45 × 10 = 84.50%.

2. CBSE Class 10th / 12th Formula (× 9.5)

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) established the multiplier of 9.5 after analysing the average percentage scored by candidates getting grade point 10 across all subjects.

Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5

Example: A CBSE CGPA of 9.20 converts to 9.20 × 9.5 = 87.40%.

3. AICTE & UGC Technical Institution Formula

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) mandates an offset calculation for B.Tech / Diploma programs to standardise percentages:

Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

Example: A CGPA of 8.00 under AICTE regulations corresponds to (8.00 - 0.75) × 10 = 72.50%.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Common questions regarding CGPA, SGPA, and Percentage conversion

What is the difference between SGPA, CGPA, and Percentage?
SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) measures academic performance in a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) represents the overall cumulative performance across all completed semesters. Percentage converts CGPA into a 100-mark relative score for comparison with traditional mark sheets.
Why do different universities have different CGPA to percentage formulas?
Each university academic council or governing body (such as UGC, AICTE, CBSE, VTU, GTU) defines its grading scheme. Some institutions scale grades linearly (×10), while others apply a fixed offset deduction (like -0.75 or -0.5) to account for internal assessment weightage and relative grading curves.
How can I convert Percentage back to CGPA?
You can switch to the "Percentage to CGPA" mode tab in our calculator. Simply enter your overall percentage and choose your university grading scale to get the exact equivalent CGPA instantly.
Is 7.5 CGPA equal to First Class with Distinction?
Yes! Under standard 10-point grading scales (such as RTU Kota, SKIT, MBM Jodhpur), a cumulative CGPA of 7.50 equates to 75.00%, which fulfills the official eligibility for First Class with Distinction provided all semesters are cleared in the first attempt.
How do foreign universities (US, UK, Canada) evaluate Indian CGPA?
Foreign universities and credential evaluation services like WES (World Education Services) convert Indian 10-point CGPA into a US 4.0 GPA scale. Generally, a 10-point CGPA of 8.0+ corresponds to ~3.5+ GPA on a 4.0 scale. Our calculator offers a built-in US 4.0 GPA conversion option for quick guidance.
