About Watermark Image Tool

The Watermark Image Tool is a free online utility that allows you to add text overlays to your images. Easily protect your work or add branding with customizable text, color, size, and positioning.

Why Watermark Images?

  • Protection: Prevent unauthorized use of your images by adding copyright text.
  • Branding: Add your logo or name to photos to increase brand visibility.
  • Information: Add dates, locations, or captions directly onto the image.

How to use?

  1. Click Upload Image to select a file from your device.
  2. Use the Watermark Settings to enter text and adjust style.
  3. Click Reset if you want to start over.
  4. Preview the result and click Download to save.
