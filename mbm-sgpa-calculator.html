Frequently Asked Questions (MBM University SGPA)

What is the official formula to convert MBM SGPA to Percentage?
As per MBM University Jodhpur CBCS regulations, the formula is Percentage (%) = SGPA × 10. For instance, an SGPA of 8.40 equals 84.00%.
What are the passing criteria for MBM B.Tech theory papers?
Students must achieve an overall aggregate of 40% combined in internal assessments and end-term examinations.
