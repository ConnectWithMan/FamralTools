About PNG to BMP Converter

The PNG to BMP Converter is a free online utility that allows you to change your PNG images into BMP files. Easily convert directly in your browser without uploading files to a server.

Why Convert PNG to BMP?

  • Lossless Quality: BMP (Bitmap) is a lossless format that stores color data for every pixel without compression, preserving exact image quality.
  • Windows Standard: It is a native format for Microsoft Windows, ensuring perfect compatibility with Windows applications and wallpapers.
  • Simple Structure: BMP files are uncompressed and easy to process, making them useful for legacy software and simple image processing tasks.

How to use?

  1. Click Upload PNG Image to select a file from your device.
  2. Click the Convert to BMP button.
  3. Preview the result and click Download to save.
