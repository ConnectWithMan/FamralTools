Step-by-Step Worked Example of SGPA Calculation

Sample Semester 3 (CSE) Grade Sheet

Let's calculate SGPA for a 3rd semester CSE student with 6 theory subjects and 3 practical labs:

  • • Data Structures & Algorithms (3 Credits) — Grade O (10 Pts) = 30.0
  • • Object Oriented Programming (3 Credits) — Grade A+ (9 Pts) = 27.0
  • • Digital Electronics (3 Credits) — Grade A (8 Pts) = 24.0
  • • Advanced Mathematics (3 Credits) — Grade B+ (7 Pts) = 21.0
  • • Software Engineering (3 Credits) — Grade A (8 Pts) = 24.0
  • • Technical Communication (2 Credits) — Grade A+ (9 Pts) = 18.0
  • • Data Structures Lab (1.5 Credits) — Grade O (10 Pts) = 15.0
  • • OOP Lab (1.5 Credits) — Grade O (10 Pts) = 15.0

Math Calculation & Result

Total Grade Points = 30 + 27 + 24 + 21 + 24 + 18 + 15 + 15 = 174.0
Total Semester Credits = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 1.5 + 1.5 = 20.0

Calculated SGPA = 174.0 / 20.0 = 8.70 / 10.00

Equivalent Percentage = 8.70 × 10 = 87.00% (First Class with Distinction)

Importance of SGPA for Engineering Placements & Higher Studies

Campus Placement Cutoffs

Top IT services and core engineering recruiters (such as TCS Digital/Ninja, Wipro, Infosys, Cognizant, L&T, Accenture, and Reliance) require a minimum cutoff of 60% (6.0 CGPA) or 65% (6.5 CGPA) with zero active backlogs. Maintaining a high SGPA in early semesters ensures you remain eligible for all campus drives.

RTU Honours Degree Eligibility

To graduate with an RTU B.Tech Honours Degree, a student must secure a cumulative CGPA of 7.50 or higher without clearing any paper in backlog attempts. Achieving consistent 8.0+ SGPAs across all 8 semesters guarantees Honours eligibility.

GATE & Higher Studies (M.Tech/MS)

Premier institutes like IITs, NITs, and foreign universities (US, Germany, UK) evaluate undergraduate transcripts based on SGPA consistency. A strong academic record in 3rd–6th semester core engineering subjects carries heavy weight during admissions.

Frequently Asked Questions (RTU SGPA)

How to convert RTU SGPA to Percentage?
According to official RTU Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) regulations, Percentage (%) = SGPA × 10. For example, an SGPA of 8.40 equals 84.00%.
What is the minimum passing mark in RTU theory exams?
Students must secure at least 35% in aggregate (midterm + endterm combined) and at least 35% in endterm university exams to earn a passing grade (Grade P or higher).
How are practical labs weighted in RTU SGPA?
Practical laboratories usually carry 1.0 or 1.5 credits each. Scoring Grade O (10 points) or A+ (9 points) in labs provides an easy boost to your total semester grade points.
What happens if I fail a subject (Grade F)?
Grade F carries 0 grade points. The subject credit is still included in the total denominator for that semester, lowering your SGPA. Once you clear the backlog in re-examinations, your grade point is updated.
