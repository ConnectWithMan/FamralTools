MBM University CGPA Formula & Degree Division Rules

1. CGPA Formula

CGPA is calculated by dividing total grade points earned across all semesters by the sum of semester credits:

CGPA = Σ (Semester SGPA × Semester Credits) / Σ (Total Credits)

2. CGPA to Percentage

The official conversion formula specified by MBM University Jodhpur regulations:

Equivalent Percentage (%) = CGPA × 10

Frequently Asked Questions (MBM CGPA)

What CGPA is required for First Class with Distinction in MBM?
A cumulative CGPA of 7.50 or higher at the end of 8 semesters awards First Class with Distinction.
How does credit weightage affect overall CGPA in MBM University?
Semesters carrying higher total credits exert a greater numerical impact on your final cumulative CGPA score.
