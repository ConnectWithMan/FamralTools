Frequently Asked Questions

What are SEO tools and why do I need them?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) tools help webmasters, developers, and marketers optimize their websites to rank higher on search engines like Google and Bing. They ensure your pages have proper meta tags, schema markup for rich results, optimized URLs, and fast loading assets.

Are all SEO tools on Famral free?

Yes, 100% free! Every tool in our SEO suite can be used without any payment, credits, or registration. You can generate unlimited Schema markup, Open Graph tags, and meta tags.

What is the difference between Meta Tags and Open Graph Tags?

Standard meta tags (title, description, robots) are used primarily by search engines like Google to display your search snippet. Open Graph tags (og:title, og:image, og:description) are used by social platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, and WhatsApp to render rich preview cards when your link is shared.

How does Schema.org JSON-LD help my website?

Schema.org structured data in JSON-LD format tells search engines precisely what your content is about (e.g., a Recipe with cook time and ratings, an FAQ, a Product with pricing, or a Local Business). Valid structured data makes your site eligible for eye-catching Google Rich Results, significantly boosting organic click-through rates (CTR).

Is my website data secure when using Famral SEO Tools?

Yes. All generation, validation, and encoding happens entirely client-side inside your web browser using modern JavaScript. No proprietary draft content or private URLs are ever uploaded, saved, or logged on our servers.
