How to Calculate SGPA & CGPA (Formulas & Rules)

1. SGPA Calculation Formula

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is computed by taking the credit-weighted sum of grade points across all registered subjects in that semester:

SGPA = Σ (Subject Credit × Grade Point) / Σ (Subject Credit)

Theory subjects carry 3 to 4 credits, while practical labs carry 1 to 2 credits.

2. CGPA Calculation Formula

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) evaluates cumulative academic performance across all completed semesters (1 to 8):

CGPA = Σ (Semester SGPA × Semester Total Credits) / Σ (Total Semester Credits)

Higher-credit semesters exert greater weight on your overall final CGPA score.

3. Official CGPA to Percentage Conversion

According to official regulations for RTU Kota, SKIT Jaipur, and MBM University Jodhpur, the formula to convert CGPA or SGPA to equivalent percentage is:

Equivalent Percentage (%) = CGPA × 10

Example: A CGPA of 8.25 corresponds directly to 82.50% marks.

Frequently Asked Questions (University Grading)

What is the official formula to convert SGPA / CGPA to Percentage?
As per official CBCS regulations issued by RTU Kota, SKIT Jaipur, and MBM University Jodhpur, the conversion formula is Percentage (%) = CGPA × 10. For example, if your CGPA is 7.80, your percentage is 78.00%.
What are the criteria for B.Tech First Class with Distinction?
A student is awarded First Class with Distinction if their cumulative CGPA is 7.50 or higher (equivalent to 75%+) at graduation without active backlogs.
How are SGPA and CGPA calculated?
SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is calculated per semester by dividing total grade points earned by total semester credits. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of all completed semester SGPAs across your degree program.
What is the passing mark requirement for theory subjects?
To pass a theory course, a student must secure at least 35% to 40% aggregate in internal midterm and end-term university examinations combined.
Do backlogs affect CGPA after clearing them?
Once you clear a backlog paper in re-examinations, your revised grade points replace Grade F (0 points), and your SGPA and CGPA are updated accordingly on your official transcript.
