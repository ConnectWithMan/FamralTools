SGPA vs CGPA: Key Differences in RTU Evaluation

Semester SGPA

  • • Measures performance in a single semester (e.g. 3rd Semester).
  • • Resets at the start of every new academic term.
  • • Formula: Σ (Course Credit × Grade Point) / Σ (Course Credit).
  • • Used for semester grade cards, re-evaluation, and semester toppers list.

Cumulative CGPA

  • • Measures cumulative performance across all 8 completed semesters.
  • • Aggregates all credits continuously throughout the 4-year degree.
  • • Formula: Σ (Semester SGPA × Semester Credits) / Σ (Total Credits).
  • • Appears on final RTU B.Tech degree certificate & official transcript.

Strategic Tips to Improve Your RTU CGPA

1. Prioritize High-Credit Semesters

Semesters 3, 4, 5, and 6 carry 24 credits each (totaling 96 credits out of ~160 degree credits). High performance in these core engineering terms has double the impact on your final CGPA compared to 8th semester (16 credits).

2. Maximize Midterm & Internal Marks

Internal assessments account for 30 out of 100 marks per subject. Securing 25+ internal marks in midterm exams and lab vivas guarantees a baseline Grade B+ or higher even with moderate end-term theory scores.

3. Score Top Grades in Practical Labs

Practical lab courses and industrial training components are easier to score Grade O (10 Pts) or Grade A+ (9 Pts). Lab grades provide a consistent buffer against tough mathematical theory papers.

Frequently Asked Questions (RTU CGPA)

What is the formula to convert RTU CGPA to Percentage?
As per official Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) regulations, Percentage (%) = CGPA × 10. For instance, a CGPA of 8.5 equals 85%.
Are all semesters weighted equally in RTU CGPA?
RTU CGPA calculation uses credit weightages. Semesters with more total credits (e.g. 3rd–6th semesters carrying 24 credits) have a higher proportional impact on your cumulative CGPA than semesters with fewer credits.
What is the minimum CGPA required for B.Tech First Class in RTU?
A student achieving a cumulative CGPA between 6.50 and 7.49 is awarded First Class Division. A CGPA of 7.50 or higher qualifies for First Class with Distinction.
Does RTU issue degree certificates with CGPA or Percentage?
RTU marksheets and provisional degree certificates display cumulative CGPA. For official job or higher education applications that require percentage, the official conversion formula (Percentage = CGPA × 10) is printed on the transcript header.
